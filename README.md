# Roundabout Rush

A side-scrolling, momentum-based cycling game. Climb, descend, and scrub speed before every roundabout on the way to the finish line.

**Play:** https://samrichardson255.github.io/roundabout-rush/

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| → | Pedal |
| ↑ | Stand and attack (burns energy; run dry and you bonk) |
| ↓ | Tuck (less drag on flats and descents) |
| ← | Brake (get under the limit before each roundabout) |
| Space | Bunny hop (clear opening car doors) |
| Esc | Back to main menu |
| M | Music on/off |

## Levels

- **Ocean Road Rollers**: coastal rollers with 4 speed-limited roundabouts.
- **PWMNR**: Mt. Newington, Post Road, The Road, Wallis Sands Sprint, and a mountain-top finish on Sagamore, plus 2 fast-cycling traffic lights (red = crash +5s, yellow = warning, green = +10% speed) and parked cars whose doors open at random.

Pick a 3-letter player name and a level from the main menu. On touch screens, swipe a letter or tap its arrows, then tap a level to ride.

## Playing on a phone

The game switches to a full-screen touch layout on phones and tablets: **Stand / Tuck / Hop** under your left thumb, **Brake / Pedal** under your right. Tap the road to bunny hop. Play in landscape.

For a true full-screen experience with no browser bars, add it to your home screen:

- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → **Add to Home Screen**, then launch it from the icon.
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**). The in-game **⛶ Full screen** button also works in the browser.

Single-file HTML5 canvas game. No build step.
